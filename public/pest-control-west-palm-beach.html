Bed Bug Prevention and Post-Treatment Care

After pest control West Palm Beach technicians complete the final inspection and confirm zero activity, property owners receive practical prevention guidance tailored to South Florida living. Regular mattress encasements rated for bed bugs create a physical barrier that prevents new infestations from taking hold. Travelers are encouraged to inspect hotel headboards and luggage racks upon arrival and to keep suitcases on hard surfaces rather than carpet.
