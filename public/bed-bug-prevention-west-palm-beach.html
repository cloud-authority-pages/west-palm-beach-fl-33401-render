Common Signs of Bed Bug Infestation

Early detection remains the most cost-effective form of bed bug prevention West Palm Beach. Residents often notice small, itchy welts arranged in lines across arms or legs after a night's sleep. Dark, rust-colored spots on sheets or mattress seams indicate fecal matter left behind by feeding adults. Shed skins resembling translucent flakes may collect along baseboards or inside dresser drawers. A sweet, musty odor becomes noticeable in heavily infested rooms, although this scent is often masked by air fresheners. Because bed bugs are nocturnal, residents may see the insects themselves only when moving furniture or lifting mattress corners. Tiny white eggs about the size of a pinhead can cluster in hidden crevices near sleeping areas. Property owners should inspect luggage after travel and examine used furniture before bringing it indoors. Regular professional inspections provide the most reliable way to catch infestations before they spread throughout an entire building or home.
