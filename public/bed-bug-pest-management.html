How does bed bug pest management work?

The process starts with a detailed inspection of sleeping areas, furniture, and baseboards to locate all bugs and eggs. Once the extent is mapped, technicians apply targeted heat or approved insecticides to cracks, crevices, and voids where bugs hide. A second visit follows seven to ten days later to catch newly hatched nymphs. Follow-up inspections confirm the infestation has been eliminated and prevent re-infestation.

How long does bed bug pest management treatment take?

An initial visit usually lasts two to four hours depending on the size of the home and the number of rooms affected. A follow-up treatment is scheduled about a week later and takes one to two hours. Most single-family homes are clear after two visits, but heavy infestations may require a third inspection to confirm no live bugs remain.

Is bed bug pest management safe for pets and children?

Only EPA-registered products labeled for indoor use near people and pets are applied. Mattresses and baseboards receive low-toxicity dusts or heat, reducing airborne residue. Pets and children must stay out of treated rooms for two hours or until surfaces are dry. Once dried, residues stay in cracks where bugs travel, keeping living spaces safe for normal activity.

What preparation is needed before bed bug pest management service?

Remove bedding and launder it on the hottest cycle. Clear floors and closets so technicians can reach baseboards. Store clothing and toys in sealed plastic bags. Move furniture a few inches from walls. Vacuum mattresses and discard the bag outside. Avoid rearranging items after preparation, since this can spread bugs to new rooms before treatment begins.
