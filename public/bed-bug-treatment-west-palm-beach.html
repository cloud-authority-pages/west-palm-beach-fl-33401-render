What signs indicate a bed bug infestation in West Palm Beach homes?

Look for small reddish-brown stains on sheets from crushed bugs, dark pepper-like droppings along mattress seams, and pale yellow shed skins in corners. Residents often notice itchy, linear bite marks on arms or legs after waking. A sweet, musty odor may also be present in heavily infested rooms. Checking behind headboards and under bed frames usually reveals the first evidence.
