Signs of Bed Bug Infestation in Your Home or Business

Recognizing bed bug activity early makes bed bug removal West Palm Beach much easier and less expensive. Small, dark, rust-colored spots on sheets, mattresses, and pillows are often the first clue homeowners notice. These spots are dried bug waste that appears where they rest during daylight hours. Live bugs are rarely seen because they hide in tiny, narrow spaces during the day. Shed skins left behind after bugs grow look like pale, translucent flakes near baseboards, headboards, and wall outlets. Bites on your body that appear in rows or clusters suggest bed bugs fed while you slept. Musty odors in bedrooms, closets, and laundry rooms can indicate a large population. West Palm Beach residents often mistake early signs for mosquito bites or allergic reactions, delaying treatment until the problem spreads. Businesses, hotels, and offices may notice complaints from guests or visitors before finding evidence themselves.
