Signs of Bed Bug Infestation You Shouldn't Ignore

Recognizing the early warning signs of a bed bug problem can prevent minor incidents from escalating into major property infestations. Many residents mistake bed bug activity for other household issues. Dark stains on mattresses or upholstered furniture often indicate bed bug feces. Shed skins appear as light-colored specks near sleeping areas. Residents may experience unexplained bite marks that appear in linear patterns on exposed skin. A musty odor sometimes emanates from heavy infestations. However, these visible signs often appear only after populations have already established in multiple locations throughout a property.
