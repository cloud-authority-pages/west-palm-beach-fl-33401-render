Signs of Bed Bug Infestation and When to Call an Exterminator

Recognizing early signs of bed bug activity allows property owners to seek professional bed bug infestation treatment in West Palm Beach before infestations become severe. Small reddish-brown stains on sheets and mattresses often indicate crushed bugs or fecal matter from feeding activity. Sweet, musty odors in sleeping areas can signal larger populations that have been present for weeks or months. Live bugs appear as flat, oval-shaped insects roughly the size of an apple seed, typically found along mattress seams, behind headboards, and in furniture joints. Property owners who notice unexplained welts or bite marks after sleeping should inspect their sleeping areas carefully before assuming other causes. Early intervention prevents the exponential growth that occurs when a few bugs reproduce over multiple generations. The CDC - Bed Bugs recommends professional evaluation whenever these signs appear, as DIY detection often misses the full extent of infestation. West Palm Beach residents should contact qualified exterminators at the first confirmed sighting rather than attempting to monitor the situation independently.
