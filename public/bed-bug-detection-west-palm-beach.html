What signs indicate a bed bug infestation in West Palm Beach?

Look for small blood stains on sheets, dark fecal spots, and itchy welts on exposed skin. Bed bugs leave dark spots from digested blood on mattresses and furniture. You may also see shed skins, small white eggs, or live bugs in mattress seams and furniture joints. Bites often appear in lines or clusters on arms, legs, and torso after waking. Early detection prevents larger problems.
