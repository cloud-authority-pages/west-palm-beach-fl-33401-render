Signs of Bed Bug Infestation in Your Home

Recognizing early signs of bed bugs allows homeowners to request residential bed bug control before populations explode. Small dark spots on sheets or mattress seams often appear first, followed by itchy welts that appear in lines or clusters on exposed skin. Live bugs measure about 1/4 inch, flat, oval, and reddish-brown in color; they hide during daylight in mattress seams, box spring folds, headboard crevices, electrical outlets, and baseboard gaps. Shed skins and tiny white eggs confirm active breeding. Musty odor becomes noticeable in heavy infestations, often compared to overripe fruit.
