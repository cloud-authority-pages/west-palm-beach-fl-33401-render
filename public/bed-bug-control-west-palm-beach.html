Signs of Bed Bugs and When to Call an Exterminator

Bed bug control West Palm Beach becomes necessary when residents notice specific warning signs that distinguish these pests from other household problems. Small blood stains on sheets often indicate feeding activity. Itchy welts appearing in linear patterns suggest multiple bites during sleep periods. Live insects measuring one-quarter inch with flat oval shapes confirm active infestations. Shed skins found near sleeping areas indicate recent development. Sweet musty odors become noticeable when infestations reach moderate to heavy density levels. Early intervention prevents widespread distribution throughout entire properties. Our experienced team can quickly identify even minor signs that might be overlooked by property owners.
